About The Studio

Rooted in Indian Culture, Designed for Global Joy

"Zingaari is an independent game development studio founded in 2025 by Dr. Mayank Loonker. We focus on creating engaging games inspired by Indian culture, everyday experiences, and local stories. Our aim is to bring distinctly Indian experiences to the world of interactive entertainment."

Distinctly Indian Identities

From lively highway dhabas with sizzling parathas to regional nuances across Punjab, Gujarat, and Maharashtra, our worlds are authentically textured.

Calm, Accessible Simulation

Casual mechanics paired with satisfying progression. We prioritize enjoyable, low-friction gameplay loops that fit naturally into players' everyday lives.

Research-Informed Design

Grounded in systematic player testing and academic design research from the National Institute of Design (NID) and University of Kent.

"Games have the power to turn everyday cultural memories into interactive adventures that make people smile." — Dr. Mayank Loonker, Founder
