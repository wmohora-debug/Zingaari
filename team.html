Leadership & Vision

Meet the Team

"Meet the people behind Zingaari, bringing together creativity, design, and development to create memorable gaming experiences."

Dr. Mayank Loonker

Founder

Design researcher, educator, and user experience practitioner who founded Zingaari in 2025 to create culturally resonant games inspired by everyday Indian life. With extensive experience in design thinking and interactive systems, Mayank guides the studio's creative vision, player psychology, and cultural narrative.

Experience Design researcher, educator & UX practitioner
Education PhD in Digital Arts – User Experience, University of Kent;
Graduate Diploma in Design, National Institute of Design (NID).
LinkedIn Profile

Prawigya Pariyar

Game Designer & Developer

AI Engineer and interactive developer who translates cultural scenarios into fluid, responsive game mechanics. Blending technical computer science engineering with new media design sensibilities, Prawigya oversees core simulation architecture, game balance, progression algorithms, and player loop feel.

Experience AI Engineer and Interactive Developer
Education B.Tech Computer Science Engineering (VIT);
Masters in New Media Design, National Institute of Design (NID Gandhinagar).
LinkedIn Profile

Sonu Krishnan

Visual Designer & Artist

Former Product Designer at Gameskraft and NIIT with dedicated game design education from NID Bengaluru. Sonu crafts the vibrant visual language, character stylization, isometric architectures, and mouthwatering food illustrations that bring Dhaba Dash's Indian aesthetic to life.

Experience Ex-Product Designer at Gameskraft, NIIT
Education Masters in Game Design, National Institute of Design (NID Bengaluru).

Studio Pedigree

Where Academic Design Thinking Meets Playful Craft

Our team brings together alumni of India’s premier design institution — the National Institute of Design (NID) — and international user experience research from the University of Kent. We don't just build game mechanics; we study how players interact with cultural symbols, comfort foods, and social rituals.
